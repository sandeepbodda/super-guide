Dr. Sandeep Bodda

Assistant Professor at the Amrita Mind Brain Center, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Amritapuri, Kerala, India. I lead the Multimodal Imaging & Neural Dynamics (MIND) Lab.

About

I earned my Ph.D. in Computational Neuroscience from the Faculty of Sciences at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham. My thesis, supervised by Dr. Shyam Diwakar, decoded hand-grasp movement from electroencephalography (EEG).

My work asks how regions of the human brain synchronise and communicate while a person moves, meditates, or holds something in mind. I use EEG to record that activity in real time and functional connectivity measures to describe the networks behind it.

My doctoral work was supported by the DST Cognitive Science Research Initiative.

Research

Functional brain networks during movement, yoga and meditation, and cognition.

Movement control and BCI

How cortical areas coordinate to plan and execute hand-grasp movements, and how those signatures can drive brain-computer interfaces.

Yoga and meditation neuroscience

How yoga and meditation practice, including Trataka, change functional connectivity and brain dynamics before and after practice.

Cognition: working memory, attention, stress

Network interactions underlying working memory load, sustained attention, and the response to stress.

MIND Lab and current studies

  • Multimodal Imaging & Neural Dynamics (MIND) Lab. The lab I lead at the Amrita Mind Brain Center, combining EEG with other imaging approaches to study how brain networks change over time.
  • Trataka meditation study. A pre/post EEG study of how Trataka (yogic gazing) practice changes brain activity and functional connectivity.
  • EEG analysis GUI. Developing a graphical tool for EEG preprocessing, microstate analysis and functional connectivity.
